      PROPOSED REAL HARDWARE ON THE TRUCK
      • Low-light HDR RGB cameras front, rear, left, right
      • LWIR thermal camera for heat signatures in darkness and haze
      • 77–81 GHz mmWave radar for distance and closing speed in fog/dust
      • LiDAR for 3D position and size
      • Visibility / environment sensor for light, humidity and particulates
      • GPS/GNSS, IMU, wheel speed (CAN bus)
      • Edge computer (NVIDIA Jetson / industrial PC), cab display, buzzer, seat vibration
      • Wi-Fi / 4G / 5G for control-room monitoring only. Driver alerts never depend on it

      MineSafe-Vision is a prototype safety-assistance system for demonstration purposes. It is not a certified vehicle control or collision-avoidance system. It does not control steering, brakes or any vehicle function.
